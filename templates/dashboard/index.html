Quick guide

A simple starting point for your project. Everything begins with a card.

01

Add your first card

Choose Add card, select a type, and give it a title. Notes hold your text; metrics and charts start as blank placeholders.

02

Find and organize your work

Open My cards to edit a card or pin a favorite. Create a collection to group related cards. Search finds titles and details.

03

Make the view work for you

Dashboard brings recent cards and collections together. Customize view hides sections you don’t need. On mobile, swipe the page links at the top.

Changes save automatically in this browser. Use Export layout in Settings to keep a copy.
